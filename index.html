The Atelier Philosophy

Born in Kurunegala.
Woven for Mindful Wanderers.

MoonLK was founded on a simple conviction: luxury should not scream; it should breathe. In our light-filled workshop on Colombo Road, Kurunegala, we weave garments that honor the equatorial heat of Sri Lanka without sacrificing structured tailoring.

Every seam is French-bound; every mother-of-pearl button is individually hand-stitched by generational seamstresses from the Wayamba province. We dye our fabrics in natural plant extractions and finish each piece in small, numbered editions.

Equatorial Linens

Dense, pre-washed weaves that drape effortlessly and soften with every island sunrise.

Mindful Small Batches

No mass overproduction. Each piece is cut and finished intentionally in Kurunegala.

Botanical Ceylon Dyes

Subtle blush and ivory tones created using native madder roots and rainwater tanks.

Fair Island Wages

Supporting regional female artisans with ethical studio employment and heritage pride.
